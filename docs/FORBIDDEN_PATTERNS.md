# FairShare — Forbidden Design Patterns & Strict Guidelines

As mandated by `specs/Design.md` Section 5 and `specs/Plan.md` Task P0-T04:

## Strictly Forbidden
1. **NO Gradients**:
   - No background gradients.
   - No purple or multi-stop gradients.
   - No card or button gradients.
   - No text gradients.
   - No decorative gradient borders or progress bars.
2. **NO Glassmorphism**:
   - No frosted glass, background blurs, or transparent blurred cards.
3. **NO Forbidden Typefaces**:
   - Do NOT use Inter, Poppins, or Montserrat.
   - Use Android-native **Roboto** default typography only.
4. **NO Forbidden Icons / AI Decorations**:
   - Do NOT use Lucide icons.
   - Do NOT use sparkles, magic-wand icons, zap/lightning icons, AI brains, robot illustrations, or blinking dots.
   - Do NOT use decorative particles or "AI magic" effects.
5. **NO Website-style Dashboards / Excessive Cards**:
   - No generic SaaS dashboard cards or floating pill containers.
   - Use clean, flat rectangular surfaces with subtle 1dp borders.

## Required Standards
- **Aesthetic**: Ink + Paper + Signal (flat neutral surfaces with warm amber #F2B84B accent and semantic financial indicators).
- **Financial Semantic Colors**: Flat positive (#55C58A), Flat negative (#E47777), Flat info (#79A9E8).
- **Shapes**: Standard 10-14dp rounded corners, 24dp for bottom sheets, 20dp for dialogs.
- **Interactions**: Android-first touch targets, bottom sheets, clean lists.
